Arrow Flow is a puzzle game that runs on your device. This policy explains what the game stores, what it does not collect, and how you can remove what it stores.

The short version

  • Arrow Flow has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features in this version, apart from the ads shown by the Android app (see below).
  • The web version shows no advertisements. The Android app shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional ads between levels, provided by Google AdMob (see “Third-party services”). The game itself uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else by the game.

Information we collect

The game does not collect personal information. In this version it does not ask for or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • advertising identifiers or other tracking identifiers (the Android app’s ad provider may use the device advertising ID; see “Third-party services”);
  • usage statistics, crash reports or analytics.

Because the game does not receive your information, the developer does not sell it, share it or use it to profile you.

What is saved on your device

To work properly, the game keeps a small amount of data in your device’s local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device:

  • Game progress: the highest level you have unlocked, whether you have cleared every level, and the arrows already cleared in a level you have not finished.
  • Daily Challenge record: the dates of the Daily Challenges you completed, the time you completed them, and whether you have seen the Daily Challenge introduction.
  • Awards: which awards you have earned and when, and the list of campaign levels counted toward them.
  • Settings: your choices for sound, vibration and reduced motion.
  • Welcome and tutorial record: which version of the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy you accepted, the date and time you accepted it, and whether you completed or skipped the tutorial.

The game uses this data only to restore your progress and to avoid showing the welcome screen and tutorial again. Nothing is saved from the welcome screen until you tap “Accept & Continue”.

Device permissions

The web version of Arrow Flow does not request any device permissions. The Android app declares the Internet and network-state permissions so that ads can load; the Google Mobile Ads SDK may declare the advertising-ID permission. None of them is used to read your contacts, files, location, camera or microphone. If your device supports it and the setting is on, the game can make a short vibration when you tap. This does not need a permission prompt and no data leaves your device. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Third-party services

The web version of Arrow Flow does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, payment, social sign-in or any other third-party service, and it does not load fonts or other content from external servers.

The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded video ads (for an extra hint) and occasional ads between levels. Google AdMob may process technical information such as your device advertising ID, IP address and device details to deliver and measure ads, under Google’s privacy policy. The final app must implement any consent and age-related settings that apply to its audience and regions. You never have to watch an ad to play or progress. Developer: have this paragraph reviewed against Google’s current data-disclosure requirements and your Google Play Data safety form before publishing, and link Google’s privacy policy.

Some things outside the game may still process technical information under their own policies:

  • If you open Arrow Flow from a website, the company that hosts that website may record standard technical details about your request, such as your IP address and browser type. Developer: name the hosting provider and confirm what it logs, or remove this point if it does not apply.
  • If you obtain Arrow Flow through an app store, the store operator handles its own data under its own terms and privacy policy. Developer: name the store(s), or remove this point if it does not apply.

Data retention and deletion

The data described above stays on your device until you remove it. The developer holds no copy, so there is nothing on a server to retrieve or delete.

You can remove it in these ways:

  • Reset progress in Settings locks all levels except level 1 again. It keeps your settings, your welcome and tutorial record, your Daily Challenge record and the awards you have earned.
  • Delete all data on this device in Me → Privacy Preferences removes everything listed above and restarts the app at the welcome screen.
  • To remove everything, clear the stored data for Arrow Flow in your device or browser settings (for example “Clear storage” or “Clear site data”), or uninstall the app if it is installed. The welcome screen and tutorial will then appear again the next time you play.

Data stored this way is also lost if you clear your browser data, change devices or reinstall, and it cannot be recovered.

Children’s privacy

Arrow Flow does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no chat and no purchases, and the web version shows no advertising. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob (see “Third-party services”). A parent or guardian can remove everything the game has stored using the steps in the section above.

Security

Your saved data lives in your device’s own storage and is protected by your device’s security, such as a screen lock. The game does not transmit it. No method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the version number above will change. When a change needs your fresh acceptance, Arrow Flow will show the welcome screen again before you continue.

Your rights

Because the game holds no personal information about you outside your own device, you can manage everything yourself as described above.
