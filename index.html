Help

How arrows exit

Tap an arrow and it slides in the direction it points. If nothing is in its way, it leaves the board. Clear every arrow to finish the puzzle.

Blocked arrows

An arrow is blocked when another arrow sits in its path. A blocked arrow will not move, and the game tells you which arrow is in the way. Clear the blocking arrow first, then try again.

Daily Challenges

Every day has one puzzle, chosen from your device’s date. Finish it to earn a virtual star. Past days can be replayed from the calendar, but a star is earned once per day.

Privacy Rights

What Arrow Flow stores

On this device only: your campaign progress, your Daily Challenge record, your awards, your settings, and a record of which Terms and Privacy Policy version you accepted. The game does not send this to the developer or anyone else.

See your data

Your progress is shown on the Me, Daily and Awards screens. The acceptance record is under Privacy Preferences.

Remove your data

Reset progress (Settings) clears your campaign. To remove everything the game has stored, use Privacy Preferences, or clear the app’s storage in your device or browser settings.

Other rights and contact

Rights that apply in your region and contact details are covered in the Privacy Policy. Contact details have not been supplied in this build yet.

Paused

Reset all progress?

Every level except level 1 will be locked again and your campaign puzzle count restarts. Awards you have already earned, Daily Challenge history and your settings stay as they are. This can't be undone.

Need another hint?

Watch a short video ad to get one extra hint for this level. It is optional, and you can keep playing without it.

Delete all data?

This removes your progress, awards, Daily Challenge history, settings and your Terms acceptance from this device. The app will restart at the welcome screen. This can't be undone.

Start a new game?

Your saved campaign progress will be erased. Earned awards and Daily Challenge history are kept.

Welcome to Daily Challenges

A new puzzle every day, based on your device’s date. Clear every arrow to earn a virtual star for that day. Stars are just for fun and have no cash value. Missed a day? You can replay it from the calendar.

Out of lives

A blocked arrow costs one heart. Restart the puzzle and look for a clear path before each tap.
